How it works

  1. The owner connects the studio's TikTok account once (Login Kit, scope user.info.basic).
  2. Only videos approved by the owner are sent to the account's TikTok inbox as drafts (Content Posting API, scope video.upload).
  3. The owner opens TikTok, reviews the draft, adds the caption, chooses privacy and posts it manually.
