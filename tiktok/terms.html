Katadesain Publisher – Terms of Service

Katadesain Publisher is an internal tool operated by Katadesain Studio. It uploads short educational videos about digital safety and technology, produced by Katadesain Studio, to Katadesain Studio's own TikTok account.

1. Who can use it

The tool is used only by Katadesain Studio to manage its own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and does not accept sign-ups from other users.

2. What it does

With the account owner's permission (TikTok Login Kit), the tool uploads finished videos to the account's TikTok inbox as drafts or, where permitted, posts them. Every video is reviewed and approved by the account owner before it is sent.

3. Content

All content uploaded is original work owned by Katadesain Studio and must follow the TikTok Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

4. Revoking access

The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security & permissions → Apps and services).

We may update these terms; the date below shows the latest version.
