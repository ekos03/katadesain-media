Katadesain Publisher – Privacy Policy

Katadesain Publisher is an internal tool of Katadesain Studio that uploads Katadesain Studio's own videos to its own TikTok account.

Data we access

Only the data needed to upload videos: the TikTok account's basic profile (open ID, display name) and an access token granted by the account owner through TikTok Login Kit.

How we use it

The access token is used solely to upload videos approved by the account owner. We do not read, collect, sell or share data of other TikTok users.

Storage & security

Tokens are stored in a private server environment, never published, and are deleted when access is revoked.

Your choices

The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security & permissions → Apps and services).

Contact

Questions: contact Katadesain Studio via Instagram @katadsain.
